# Architecture

`frontend/`: React + TypeScript + Vite, Tailwind, local UI components following shadcn conventions, React Flow timelines and Recharts. Same-origin `/api` requests through the development proxy. No browser calls to external services.

`backend/app/`: FastAPI validation and local-only request guards; SQLAlchemy + SQLite persistence; independent NumPy simulation engine; replaceable template narrative provider. Versioned SQL migrations run at startup. Every completed run stores starting state, input profile, assumptions, decisions, seed, engine version, monthly snapshots, events and narrative reports. No run-update endpoint exists. Rewind creates a new scenario with a copied historical state; parent records are preserved.

## Model (USD, nominal)

Monthly business revenue compounds at configured annual growth / 12, plus expansion growth. Operating costs scale with revenue at the starting cost ratio; manager salary and fixed obligations are additional. Positive profit pays an effective tax rate. Negative business cash flow is funded by personal cash. Personal spending inflates monthly. Investments compound at annual return / 12. Positive cash flow is split between cash and investments at the allocation rate; a cash deficit liquidates investments before becoming unfunded debt. Debt accrues interest. Net worth = cash + investments + other assets − debts. Business sale value is deliberately excluded. Spending and expenses are never counted twice. Financial independence uses investments × withdrawal assumption against annual personal spending; it excludes business income and home equity.

Work hours change with explicit actions. Weekly work + sleep + family + exercise + travel + personal time must fit 168 hours. The engine preserves sleep, caps work to available time and scales expansion growth by available owner capacity. It reports constraints, never predicts illness or others' emotions. Manager hiring is persistent and cannot be charged twice; expansion starts once. Scheduled synthetic business disruptions occur every 12 months starting at month 6, costing the configured fraction of monthly revenue. An expense surprise option funds the repair from the same cash ledger.

Deterministic paths have no random shocks. Monte Carlo uses independent normal monthly log revenue shocks and log investment shocks, with explicitly configurable annual volatility. The same seed, run count, and absolute month use common random draws across decisions. Conditional modeled 10th/50th/90th final-net-worth percentile **representative paths** are unfavorable/central/favorable, not real-world likelihoods or independently assembled percentile snapshots. Two passes avoid retaining 10,000 full histories. Sensitivity reruns vary business growth and investment return by two percentage points, one at a time. This is a simplified educational model: no asset tax lots, progressive taxation, FX, business valuation, borrowing facilities, or specific medical predictions.

Profile fields carry provenance (fictional, user assumption, or observed); decisions are hypothetical. Actual observations are stored separately and never automatically change a historical run. CSV profile exchange uses a strict `field,value` format with JSON values, including nested businesses/accounts/goals. Imports are validated with Pydantic, bounded in size and never evaluated as code. JSON workspace export includes historical scenarios and runs for inspection; profile JSON/CSV imports create a new profile.

## Security boundary

Bind to loopback, reject foreign Origin/Host headers and oversized requests, no permissive CORS. Static scripts, styles and fonts are local. No analytics, external AI or synchronization. There is no multi-user authentication: do not change bind address or publish. Data export contains personal records by design; application logs do not log request bodies. Explicit deletion removes all local records; restore of complete workspace backups is not implemented.
